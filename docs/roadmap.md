# Business World Roadmap

## Current checkpoint
The GitHub project is now initialized as a browser-based Three.js city simulation.

The first checkpoint includes:
- 3D island surrounded by ocean
- connected street grid with sidewalks and lane markings
- autonomous workers: Reynaldo, Moshe and Victor
- moving traffic
- Marketing Studio, Accounting Hub, Headquarters, Think Tank, apartments, café, market and school
- central Business Growth monument with live metrics
- day / dusk / night modes
- rain mode
- second airport island
- second island begins gray/locked and unlocks at $10,000 revenue
- bridge appears/animates when the airport island unlocks
- mobile camera controls and tappable buildings

## Art direction
Keep the polished colorful isometric city-builder look:
- bright readable buildings with distinct silhouettes
- rocky island edges, beaches, trees and landscaping
- water all the way to the edge of the world
- realistic road connectivity with no roads ending in grass
- central park/monument as the visual anchor
- avoid flat placeholder-looking blocks wherever possible
